# Fabio Valentini Trading Logic Masterclass

## 1. Core Setups

### A. The "AAA" Setup (Trend Following)
*   **Context**: Low on the curve (Value Area Low) in a session that shows early signs of accumulation/absorption.
*   **Trigger**:
    *   Sellers try to push below Value Area Low (VAL).
    *   **Absorption**: Big sell orders hit a wall of passive buy orders (visible in Orderflow/Footprint).
    *   Sellers get exhausted/absorbed.
*   **Action**: Enter LONG at the bottom of the range.
*   **Target**: Value Area High (VAH) or Session High.
*   **Stop Loss**: Below the absorption level (tight stop).
*   **Quote**: "Value area low ... sellers super aggressive but ... being absorbed. This is the AAA setup."

### B. Momentum Trade (Breakout/Squeeze)
*   **Context**: Price is expanding, breaking key levels (e.g., VAH, Previous Session High).
*   **Trigger**:
    *   Aggressive buying at the top.
    *   Use a **"Protection Level"**: Identify where big buyers stepped in to protect the price.
    *   Enter on the breakout or slight retracement to the protection level.
*   **Management**:
    *   **Dynamic Risk**: Trail stop loss aggressively. "If I'm right, I cover myself fast."
    *   Scale in: Add contracts as the move confirms, covering stops at break-even or profit.
*   **Quote**: "I'm betting on a long session and I'm fast in covering myself... capitalizing on the squeeze."

### C. Failed Auction (Reversal/Fade)
*   **Context**: Price tries to break a level (e.g., VAH) but fails to hold or rotate.
*   **Trigger**:
    *   Price breaks out but stalls.
    *   "Trapped Buyers": Aggressive buying at the top with no follow-through.
    *   Sellers take back control and push back into the range.
*   **Action**: Short (Fade) back to the middle or other side of the range.
*   **Target**: Value Area Low or Mid-range.
*   **Quote**: "They went below the structure... completely failed. Huge absorption. I can put all my stop loss above this."

## 2. Market Mechanics & Tools

*   **Absorption**: High volume traded at a specific price without price moving in that direction (e.g., high sell volume but price doesn't drop = Buy Wall).
*   **Protection Levels**: Areas where "Big Trades" (institutions) stepped in to defend a position.
*   **Value Area**: The core reference frame. Trading from VAL to VAH (Balance) or Breakout from VAH (Imbalance).
*   **P-Shape Profile**: Accumulation at the top (Power Hour setup). "Rebalance till power hour... then expand."

## 3. Risk Management & Psychology

*   **Daily Drawdown Limit**: Strict limit (e.g., $10k on a large account).
*   **Cushion Building**:
    *   Start small.
    *   Use profit from the first trade to finance risk on the second. "Risking back our profit."
    *   "Sit on the cushion." If you are up $20k, worst case is you finish flat or small green. Don't turn a big green day red.
*   **Asymmetrical Risk**:
    *   Look for 1:4 or 1:5 trades.
    *   **Cut losers fast**: "When you are wrong, you are wrong fast."
    *   Don't hold through deep drawdowns.
*   **Walking Away**: "We took another 3,600. Bam. $24,300. We are done in 20 minutes." (Don't overtrade).

## 4. Execution Nuances
*   **Range Charts**: Uses "40 Range" charts for New York Open to see structure clearly without noise.
*   **Speed**: "You need to be fast... decision process must be instant."
*   **Orderflow**: "Price action is the result. Orderflow is what drives the price."
