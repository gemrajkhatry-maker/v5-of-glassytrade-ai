## 2. Step-by-Step Trade Processing

### Phase 1: Market State Assessment
- Checks if market is **balanced** (rotating around POC, overlapping candles, failed VA breaks) or **imbalanced** (displacement leg, acceptance outside VA)
- Uses previous session's volume profile as the baseline
- **Key rule**: If state is unclear → stay flat, don't guess

### Phase 2: Level Identification
- Marks **POC** (magnet for mean reversion, first target for trend trades)
- Marks **VAH/VAL** (balance boundaries)
- Profiles the impulse leg that broke structure and marks **LVNs** inside it
- LVNs are "reaction zones" — where he expects pullbacks to stall
- Places **alerts** at levels, never blind limit orders

### Phase 3: Aggression Confirmation (The Critical Gate)
This is the part that differs most between his ideal setup and what's available in Indian markets:

**With full order flow data (CME/Nasdaq via Deep Charts):**
- Filters for **"Big Trades"** — isolates large institutional prints that significantly move price
- Looks for **stacked imbalances** — 3+ consecutive price bins leaning same direction (institutional pressure building)
- Watches **Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD)** — confirms directional pressure
- Detects **absorption** — high volume + low price movement = institutions absorbing aggressive orders
- Waits for **delta flip** — when a zone previously dominated by one side gets reclaimed by the other

**Without full order flow (your India proxy approach):**
- Uses a "confirmation bundle" requiring **at least 2 of 3**:
  1. Tight spread (liquidity present)
  2. Volume impulse vs recent average
  3. Rising OI + bid/ask imbalance

## 3. Entry Execution

### Setup A — Trend Continuation (matches your Setup 1)
```
Market out of balance → Displacement leg identified → Profile the leg →
Mark LVNs → Wait for pullback INTO LVN → Confirm aggression at LVN →
Enter in trend direction
```
- **Avoids first touch** if the move is too sharp
- Starts with **smaller position**, adds if market confirms
- Every position has a **predetermined invalidation point**

### Setup B — Mean Reversion / Failed Breakout (matches your Setup 2)
```
Market in balance → Price breaks out → Fails to hold (no acceptance) →
Reclaims back inside balance → Pullback to reclaim-leg LVN →
Confirm aggression in snap-back direction → Enter
```
- "If wrong, it should be wrong immediately"
- **Never widens stop loss**

### Setup C — Triple-A Setup (Absorption → Accumulation → Aggression)
*Not in your playbook but a core Fabio pattern:*
```
1. Absorption detected (high volume, low range — big players building passively)
2. Accumulation phase (price contracts into tight range as positions build)
3. Aggression breakout (volume surge as institutional move initiates)
→ Enter on the aggression phase breakout
```

## 4. Stop Loss Placement

- Just beyond the confirming rejection swing / absorption area
- Buffer: 1–2 ticks (adjust for instrument)
- **Break-even rule**: If CVD strongly favors the trade → move SL to break-even early
- If confirmation disappears quickly (no follow-through) → scratch/exit early
- **NEVER widen SL** — invalidation is instant

## 5. Trade Management & Exit (The Most Detailed Part)

Fabio's exit process is **multi-layered**, not a simple target hit:

### Partial Profit Taking
| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| First opposing order-flow signal | Scale out partial position |
| +1R gain reached | Take partial profit |
| +1.5R gain | Trail stop to VWAP band |

### Target Rules
- **Primary target** = Prior balance POC (exit full position by default)
- **Only allow a runner** if:
  - Day remains strongly trending
  - Pullbacks stay shallow
  - Spreads remain tight
- **Secondary targets** = VAH or VAL (depending on direction)

### Abort Signals (Kill the trade)
- Aggressive unwind shown by **delta divergence**
- Opposing **stacked imbalances** appear
- Clean **VWAP reclaim** against trade bias
- Pressure fades — no follow-through

## 6. Risk Management Rules

| Rule | Value |
|------|-------|
| Risk per trade | 0.25% – 0.5% of account |
| Max stop-outs per day | **3** (then stop trading) |
| Position sizing | In lot multiples, capped by notional + liquidity |
| Daily loss limit | Defined before session starts |

**3-loss rule**: If you hit 3 stop-outs, the market isn't aligned with your reads → **stop trading for the day**.

## 7. Timing Rules

- **Skip first 5–30 minutes** after open (spreads + fake moves)
- **Avoid last 5–10 minutes** before close
- For MCX: avoid thin liquidity windows in late hours
- Trade with the **prevailing daily trend** as default
