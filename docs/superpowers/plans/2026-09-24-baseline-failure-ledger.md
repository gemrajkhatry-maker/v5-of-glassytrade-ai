## Gate rules

1. A safety row cannot be marked resolved by deleting or weakening its assertion.
2. A target-wave implementation must link its evidence command to this ledger and preserve the pre-refactor result until the replacement gate passes.
3. The root-suite lifecycle timeout is a release blocker, not a flaky-test excuse.
4. Broker live tests remain deselected unless the deployment owner explicitly supplies isolated credentials, database, and cutover evidence.
5. This ledger is append-only until a reviewed target wave records a replacement row and its evidence artifact.
